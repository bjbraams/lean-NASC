26 Hybrid Methods
260 Historical introduction

The idea of combining the ideas behind Runge~Kutta methods, with those
behind linear multistep methods, dates from the period 1960-1970. One
approach is to make use of stage derivatives computed in one or more previous
steps in the computation of the approximation at the end of a current step.
Methods based on this idea are referred to as pseudo Runge-Kutta methods.
The earliest work on these methods is that of Byrne and Lambert (1966).
