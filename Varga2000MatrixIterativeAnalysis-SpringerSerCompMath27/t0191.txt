6. Derivation and Solution of Elliptic

6.1 A Simple Two-Point Boundary-Value Problem

The basic aims in this chapter are to derive finite difference approximations
for certain elliptic differential equations, to study the properties of the asso-
ciated matrix equations, and to describe rigorous iterative methods for the
solution of such matrix equations. In certain cases, the derived properties
of the associated matrix equations are sufficiently powerful to allow us to
prove rather easily (Theorem 6.2) that these finite difference approximations
become more accurate with finer mesh spacings, but results in this area, es-
pecially in higher dimensions, require detailed developments which we omit.
Rather, in this chapter we concentrate on useful methods for deriving finite
difference approximations of boundary-value problems which apply to inter-
nal interfaces, which are of interest in reactor physics.
