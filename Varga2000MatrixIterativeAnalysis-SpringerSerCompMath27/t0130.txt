It is instructive at this point to reconsider the numerical example of Sect.
3.1. There, both the point Jacobi and point Gauss-Seidel iterative methods
were applied to a particular equation Ax = k. As this matrix A is a con-
sistently ordered 2-cyclic matrix (see Exer. 3 of Sect. 4.1), we conclude the-
oretically that the asymptotic rate of convergence of the point Gauss-Seidel
iterative method is twice that of the point Jacobi iterative method. But we
can observe this numerically from (3.24), where the error vectors for these
