rise to consistent orderings are not unique.

The basic reason for introducing the concepts of a p-cyclic matrix, and
consistent orderings for such matrices, is that the knowledge of these ma-
trix properties greatly facilitates the theoretical analysis of the successive
overrelaxation iterative method, which will be treated in Sect. 4.2 and Sect.
4.3. Fortunately, as we shall see in Chap. 6, these matrix properties are not
difficult to determine.

The distinction between consistent and inconsistent orderings is theoreti-
cally important when one considers, as in Sect. 4.4, the effect that orderings
have on asymptotic rates of convergence. The nature of consistent orderings
will be more fully explored for the case p = 2 in Sect. 4.4, where again we
will find graph theory to be both convenient and instructive.
