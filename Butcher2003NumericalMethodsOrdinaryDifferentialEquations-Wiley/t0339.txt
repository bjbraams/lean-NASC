A second procedure for determining stability regions is based on the idea
of the ‘type of a polynomial’. That is, if P is a polynomial of degree n then
the type is a triple (n1,n2,n3), where ni, na and ng are non-negative integers
with sum exactly n. The interpretation is that 1 is the number of zeros of P
in the open unit disc, nz is the number of zeros on the unit circle and ng is the
number of zeros outside the closed unit disc. If we are willing to concentrate on
the open stability region of a specific method, we can simplify the discussion
to the question of determining whether or not the type of P is (n,0,0). We
