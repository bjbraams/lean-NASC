Although A-stable Runge-Kutta methods can have arbitrarily high orders,
the order of A-stable linear multistep methods is restricted to 2. This was first
proved using order stars (Wanner, Hairer and Nogrsett, 1978), but we prefer
to use the closely related approach of order arrows (Butcher, 2002). These will
be introduced in the Riemann surface case in the next subsection.
