Historically, Perron (1907) proved this theorem assuming that A > O.
Whereas this obviously implies that the matrix A is irreducible in this case,
however, Perron showed that there was but one eigenvalue of modulus p(A).
Later, Frobenius (1912) extended most of Perron’s results to the class of
nonnegative irreducible matrices. In the next section, we shall investigate
precisely how many eigenvalues of a nonnegative irreducible n x n matrix can
have their moduli equal to p(A).
