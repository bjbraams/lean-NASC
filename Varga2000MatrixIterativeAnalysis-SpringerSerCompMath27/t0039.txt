2.1 Spectral Radii of Nonnegative Matrices

In investigations of the rapidity of convergence of various iterative meth-
ods, the spectral radius p(A) of the corresponding iteration matrix A plays
a major role. For the practical estimation of this constant p(A), we have
thus far only the upper bounds for p(A) of Sect. 1.4, provided by extensions
of Gerschgorin’s Theorem 1.11. In this section, we shall look closely into the
Perron-Frobenius theory of square matrices having nonnegative real numbers
as entries. Not only will this theory provide us with both nontrivial upper and
lower bounds for the spectral radius for this class of matrices, but the struc-
ture of this theory will be decidedly germane to our subsequent development
of iterative methods. We begin with
