10 1. Matrix Properties and Concepts

In the terminology of Faddeev and Faddeeva (1963) and Householder
(1958), (1.23) states that the spectral norm of a matrix is consistent with the
Euclidean vector norm, and (1.24) states that the spectral norm of a matrix
is subordinate to the Euclidean vector norm. There are many other ways of
defining vector and matrix norms that satisfy the properties of Theorems 1.2
and 1.5. Although some other definitions of norms are given in the exercises
for this section, we have concentrated only on the Euclidean vector norm and
the matrix spectral norm, as these will generally be adequate for our future
purposes.
