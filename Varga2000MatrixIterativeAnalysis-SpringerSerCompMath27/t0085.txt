Thus, the Stein-Rosenberg theorem gives us our first comparison theo-

not only is the point Gauss-Seidel iterative method computationally more
convenient to use (because of storage requirements) than the point Jacobi
iterative method, but it is also asymptotically faster when the Jacobi matrix
B is nonnegative with 0 < p(B) <1.
