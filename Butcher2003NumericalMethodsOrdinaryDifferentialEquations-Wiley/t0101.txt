234 Introduction to order conditions

As the order being sought increases, the algebraic conditions, on the
coefficients of the method, become increasingly complicated. The pattern
behind these conditions is known and, in this brief introduction to the
order conditions, we state the results without any justification and show, by
examples, how they are used.
