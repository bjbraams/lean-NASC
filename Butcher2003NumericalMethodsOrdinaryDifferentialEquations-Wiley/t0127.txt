114 NUMERICAL METHODS FOR ORDINARY DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS

Methods of various orders have been derived by Rosenbrock and others.
These are known collectively as Rosenbrock methods, although the ambiguous
name ‘implicit Runge-Kutta methods’ is sometimes applied to them.
