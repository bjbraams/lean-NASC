## Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computing

A bibliography of graduate textbooks, research monographs, and authoritative review articles in numerical analysis and scientific computing, selected to define the core mathematical theory and support formalization in Lean. Emphasis is placed on modern, theorem-driven treatments whose functional-analytic, matrix-analytic, and approximation-theoretic formulations provide precise statements for later formalization.

### Core Graduate Textbooks and Functional-Analytic Foundations

* Kendall Atkinson and Weimin Han, *Theoretical Numerical Analysis: A Functional Analysis Framework*, 3rd ed., Texts in Applied Mathematics 39, Springer, 2009. ISBN 978-1-4419-0457-7 (print), 978-1-4419-0458-4 (eBook). [DOI 10.1007/978-1-4419-0458-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4419-0458-4). Develops approximation, operator perturbation, Galerkin and collocation projection methods, variational inequalities, and integral and elliptic equations in Banach and Hilbert spaces.
* Alfio Quarteroni, Riccardo Sacco, and Fausto Saleri, *Numerical Mathematics*, 2nd ed., Texts in Applied Mathematics 37, Springer, 2007. ISBN 978-3-540-34658-6 (print). [DOI 10.1007/b98885](https://doi.org/10.1007/b98885). Broad graduate course spanning numerical linear algebra, rootfinding, interpolation, quadrature, optimization, and ordinary and boundary-value differential problems.
* Walter Gautschi, *Numerical Analysis*, 2nd ed., Birkhäuser, 2012. ISBN 978-0-8176-8258-3 (print), 978-0-8176-8259-0 (eBook). [DOI 10.1007/978-0-8176-8259-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-8176-8259-0). Concise graduate text with particularly thorough treatments of machine arithmetic, conditioning, polynomial and spline approximation, Gaussian quadrature, and ordinary differential equations.
* Peter Deuflhard and Andreas Hohmann, *Numerical Analysis in Modern Scientific Computing: An Introduction*, 2nd ed., Texts in Applied Mathematics 43, Springer, 2003. ISBN 978-0-387-95410-3 (print), 978-0-387-21584-6 (eBook). [DOI 10.1007/978-0-387-21584-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-21584-6). Emphasizes conditioning, affine-invariant Newton methods, orthogonalization, three-term recurrences, Chebyshev and B-spline approximation, and adaptive quadrature.
* Josef Stoer and Roland Bulirsch, *Introduction to Numerical Analysis*, 3rd ed., Texts in Applied Mathematics 12, Springer, 2002. ISBN 978-0-387-95452-3 (print), 978-0-387-21738-3 (eBook). [DOI 10.1007/978-0-387-21738-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-21738-3). Classical graduate treatise with complete proofs on error propagation, interpolation, extrapolation, linear systems, eigenvalue algorithms, and multistep and shooting methods.
* Philippe G. Ciarlet, *Linear and Nonlinear Functional Analysis with Applications*, SIAM, 2013. ISBN 978-1-61197-258-0 (print), 978-1-61197-259-7 (eBook). [DOI 10.1137/1.9781611972597](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611972597). Connects Banach and Sobolev space theory, Lax–Milgram and Babuška–Brezzi theorems, monotone operators, and calculus of variations directly to variational numerical analysis.

Fixed-point theorems, constructive finite element error constants, and spectral bounds for rigorous computer-assisted existence proofs in nonlinear elliptic PDEs.
